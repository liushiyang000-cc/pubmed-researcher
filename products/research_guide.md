# PubMed Research Guide
## How to Search PubMed Like a Pro

### Basic Search
1. Go to https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov
2. Enter keywords
3. Click Search

### Advanced Search
1. Use MeSH terms
2. Filter by date
3. Limit to human studies

### Export Results
1. Click Send to
2. Select Citation Manager
3. Download .nbib file

### Tips
- Use Boolean operators (AND, OR, NOT)
- Filter by article type
- Sort by date or relevance
